## 1. Scope of Certification

This certificate certifies the AIS Domain Baseline v1.0, encompassing:

- **Constitutional Framework**: 23 Constitutional Principles (CP-001–CP-023), 12 Architectural Laws (AL-001–AL-012)
- **Architecture Baseline**: 8 Functional Pillars, 11 Design Requirements, 5 Trust Zones, 5 Autonomy Levels
- **Domain Model**: 20 entities, 6 aggregates, 4 FSMs, 9 value objects, 13 domain events, 14 invariants
- **Decision Records**: 14 Architecture Decision Records (ADR-001–ADR-014)
- **Governance**: Decision Governance Model, Domain Governance Standard, Acceptance Decision
- **Ubiquitous Language**: 18 terms, 6 categories, 15 domain principles

## 6. Conditions for Continued Validity

This certificate remains valid provided:

1. No modifications are made to the 24 constituent documents without a formal change request per GOV-003.000

4. All changes are documented in a successor certification report
