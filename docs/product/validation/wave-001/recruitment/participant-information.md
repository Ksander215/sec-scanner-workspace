# Participant Information — Pre-Session

This document is sent to accepted participants BEFORE their session.

---

## What You Need to Know

### Purpose

We are studying how people understand the architecture of an unfamiliar software system when given different types of support.

> We are evaluating a tool, not evaluating you. There are no wrong answers.

### Session Format

The session has two phases:

1. **Independent exploration** — you explore the codebase on your own and answer an architecture question
2. **Tool-assisted exploration** — you use an analysis tool to answer the same question

After both phases, you will be asked to verify the tool's claims and share your experience.

### Time & Logistics

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Duration | 90–120 minutes |
| Format | Individual session (online or in-person) |
| Timebox for independent phase | 45 minutes |
| What to bring | Nothing special — just yourself |

### What You Will Work With

- A TypeScript codebase (~393 files, ~73k lines of code)
- Standard development tools (code editor, terminal, file browser)
- An analysis tool (provided during the second phase)

### Your Task

You will be asked an architecture question about the codebase. Your goal is to understand the system's structure well enough to answer the question and support your answer with evidence from the code.

### What We Record

- Your answers (written or verbal)
- Files you open and search
- Time taken for each phase
- Your verification of the tool's claims
- Your feedback in a short interview

### What We Do NOT Record

- API keys, credentials, or secrets
- Private personal information
- Your real name (we use anonymous IDs)

### Before the Session

- No preparation is required
- Do not research the project beforehand
- Do not look up the specific project online
- Come as you would to any normal work task

### During the Session

- Think aloud as much as possible — it helps us understand your reasoning
- If you get stuck, that's okay — it's valuable information
- If you finish early, let the observer know
- You may withdraw at any time without explanation

### After the Session

- Your data will be anonymized
- You may request deletion of your data
- You will not be identified in any report
