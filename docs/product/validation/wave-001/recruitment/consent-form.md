## Pre-Session Consent Summary

Before your session, please be aware:

1. **Voluntary** — you may withdraw at any time
2. **Anonymized** — we use participant IDs, not real names
3. **No risk** — no technical, personal, or professional risk
4. **Recording** — your answers and actions will be recorded for analysis
5. **Tool evaluation** — we evaluate the tool, not you
6. **No preparation needed** — come as you would to any work task

Full consent will be obtained at the session start before any activity begins.
